# Procurement Policy

## Competitive Bidding Thresholds

All procurement activities must follow these thresholds:

- Purchases under $5,000: Single quote acceptable
- Purchases $5,000 - $25,000: Minimum of three quotes required
- Purchases over $25,000: Formal competitive bidding process required

## Travel Expense Policy

Travel expenses must be supported by the following documentation:

- Flight tickets: Original boarding passes and e-ticket receipts
- Hotel accommodation: Itemized hotel bills showing room rate and dates
- Per diem: Travel authorization form approved before travel
- Local transportation: Receipts for amounts over $25

Maximum allowable rates:
- Domestic flights: Economy class only
- International flights: Business class allowed for flights over 6 hours
- Hotel accommodation: Up to $150 per night for domestic travel
- Meals per diem: $50 per day

## Gift Policy

Employees may accept gifts under the following conditions:

- Gifts valued under $50: Acceptable if occasional and not influencing business decisions
- Gifts valued $50-$100: Must be reported to supervisor
- Gifts over $100: Must be declined or donated to charity
- Cash gifts: Never acceptable under any circumstances

## Segregation of Duties - Cash Handling

To maintain proper internal controls:

- Cash receipts must be recorded by one person and deposited by another
- Bank reconciliations must be performed by someone independent of cash handling
- Approval authority must be separate from payment processing
- Physical access to cash must be limited to authorized personnel only

## Audit Documentation Requirements

All audit work must be documented with:

- Working papers showing audit procedures performed
- Evidence of supervisory review on all workpapers
- Clear cross-references between workpapers and audit findings
- Retention of audit files for minimum 7 years

## Donor Compliance - USAID

For USAID-funded projects:

- All costs must be allocable, allowable, and reasonable
- Unallowable costs include: alcoholic beverages, entertainment, lobbying
- Time and effort reporting required for all personnel charged to grants
- Prior approval required for budget reallocations over 10%
